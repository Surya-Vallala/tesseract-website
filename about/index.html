About Tesseract Studio

A design-build practice in Hyderabad, led by people who care about the details.

Surya Vallala

The Founder

With a deep passion for creating spaces that resonate with the people who inhabit them, Surya founded Tesseract Studio to bridge the gap between impersonal design and truly personalized living.

Having honed his craft working with reputed architects and builders, Surya brings both the design sensibility and execution discipline that define Tesseract’s approach.

Shivani Patil

Lead Architect

As the Lead Architect at Tesseract Studio, Shivani joined the team in early 2022, bringing a fresh, modern perspective that defines the firm’s aesthetic. With a keen eye for contemporary design and a talent for innovation, she leads the entire design team, transforming abstract concepts into sophisticated, livable realities. Her ability to merge creative flair with functional design ensures that every project at Tesseract Studio remains at the forefront of modern architectural excellence.
